# Disney Mayhem Design Rules

## Visual Direction

- iOS-inspired
- calm
- premium
- playful but not childish
- optimized for outdoor readability

## Theme

- Use a true black app base.
- Use dark elevated/frosted surfaces sparingly.
- Use bright accent colors only for actions, highlights, and status.
- Do not use large bright color fields.

## Layout

- Mobile-first.
- Keep content width constrained on desktop.
- Prefer stacked utility layouts over heavy card grids.
- Use subtle dividers for scanability.
- Land cards may use contained frosted card styling.
- Timeline rows should stay compact and readable.

## Controls

- Use pill buttons and icon buttons.
- Tap targets must be at least 44px.
- Use Lucide icons only.
- Keep edit/add/status controls visually quiet.
- Keep visible focus states.

## Today Screen

Today should feel immediately understandable:

- NOW is the strongest focal point.
- NEXT is secondary.
- LATER is a compact utility list.
- Full timeline belongs on the day timeline page, not Today.

## Land Cards

- Land cards render inside timeline blocks.
- One contained card per land section is acceptable.
- Do not turn the full time block into one oversized card.
- Do not duplicate land cards elsewhere on the page.
